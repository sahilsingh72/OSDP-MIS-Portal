4.  How to record the short video
Why: the video is proof that the session really took place. It does not need to be perfect, but it must be clear.
Length
About 1 minute. Not less than 30 seconds and not more than 2 minutes. Record one clip only.
Phone position
Hold the phone sideways, so the screen is wide like a TV. This is also called landscape. Do not record with the phone upright.
When
In the middle of the session, while the trainer is teaching or the participants are doing an activity. Not before the start and not after the end.
Where to stand
At the back or the side corner of the room, so that the trainer, the participants and the training banner can all be seen.
How to hold
Hold the phone with both hands and keep it steady. Move slowly from one side of the room to the other. Do not zoom in on the face of any one participant.
Sound
Keep the trainer's voice clear. Stand away from fans and doors. Do not ask participants to pose or to say their names.
Quality setting
Set the phone camera to HD 720p or 1080p. Do not use 4K, because the file becomes too large to upload.
Check
Play the video once before leaving the venue. Is the picture clear, is the voice audible, and is the whole group visible? If not, record again.
File
Keep it as MP4. Name it with the tier, place and date, for example Block_Kantapada_07-10-2026.mp4. Then upload it to the batch in the MIS.
Do not photograph or record registers, files or screens with beneficiary data. Length, size limits and file type are our proposal. Please confirm with OCAC.

6.  Simple rules
1.  
Send everything within 48 hours. The MIS flags any batch whose pack is late or incomplete.
2.  
Do not photograph registers, files or screens with beneficiary data. Participant names on the attendance sheet are personal data. Keep the paper pack safe and give it only to the District Coordinator.
3.  
Check photos, video and scans are clear before leaving the venue. Redo them there if not.
4.  
No internet at the venue? Save the files on the phone and upload when you are back in coverage. Do not skip the upload.
5.  
Do not throw away paper originals until the District Coordinator confirms the pack is accepted.
