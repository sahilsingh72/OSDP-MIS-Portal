3. Summary  Information  
3.1 About the Engagement  
OCAC, the Technical Directorate of the E&IT Department, Government of Odisha, on behalf of the 
Department of Health & Family Welfare, Government of Odisha, invites proposals (“Tenders”) in 
response to this Request for Proposal (“RFP”) from eligible Service  Provider firms (“Bidders”) for 
undertaking Capacity Building, Training, and Awareness on the Odisha State Data Policy (OSDP v2.0), 
as detailed in the Terms of Reference of this RFP. 
 
Proposals must be submitted within the time, date, and venue specified in the Fact Sheet. Any proposal 
received after the prescribed deadline will be rejected and will not be considered under this procurement 
process. 
 
OCAC will award the Contract to the successful Bidder whose proposal is evaluated as the best value 
based on the prescribed Technical and Financial evaluation criteria and is approved by the Tender 
Accepting Authority. OCAC also reserves the right to select more than one firm to undertake the required 
scope of work. 
 
3.2 Background & Objective 
 
OCAC, leads and supports key digital governance initiatives across the State. As Odisha advances its 
digital transformation agenda, strengthening data governance and improving technology -enabled 
service delivery have become central priorities. Building the  capacities of government officials and 
frontline functionaries is critical to achieving these goals. 
Odisha State Data Policy  2.0 aims to establish standardized data governance practices, safeguard 
privacy, enable interoperability, and enhance transparency and efficiency in the delivery of welfare 
schemes. 
To ensure effective implementation and sustained utilization of these initiatives, OCAC intends to 
engage a competent agency to design and deliver structured training, capacity-building, and awareness 
programmes at the Directorate, District, Block, and Gram Panchayat levels. 
3.2.1 Context 
3.2.1.1 Odisha State Data Policy (OSDP) 2.0 
Government departments generate and manage significant volumes of citizen and scheme -related 
data. The Odisha State Data Policy  2.0 provides a structured framework to ensure that such data is 
collected, stored, shared, and used in a secure, ethical, and standardized manner.  
The Policy lays down guidelines covering: 
• Data collection and storage 
• Data quality and standardization

• Data sharing and interoperability 
• Privacy protection and ethical use 
• Access control and approvals 
• Risk mitigation 
• Retention and disposal 
It also defines roles and responsibilities of officials at different administrative levels and prescribes 
processes to ensure accountability and compliance. Effective implementation requires that officers and 
data handlers understand data governance principles, follow prescribed procedures, and integrate them 
into routine operations. 
Capacity building on OSDP is therefore essential to promote responsible data management, improve 
data quality, and strengthen service delivery outcomes across departments.  
 
3.3 Need for the Assignment 
Successful policy adoption and platform utilization depend on structured, large -scale training and 
awareness efforts covering: 
• Directorate level senior officials 
• District administrators 
• Block-level officers and data handlers 
• Gram Panchayat-level frontline staff 
• Citizens accessing welfare schemes 
This RFP seeks to establish a standardized, scalable training framework to ensure consistent 
messaging, measurable improvements in knowledge and system usage, and sustainable long -term 
adoption across Odisha. 
3.4 Purpose of the RFP 
Through this RFP, OCAC aims to select a qualified agency to: 
a. Design and deliver a comprehensive Statewide Training Programme on the Odisha State Data 
Policy 2.0, including practical exercises and implementation support. 
b. Develop bilingual training modules, SOPs, reference materials, and digital learning resources.  
c. Establish a monitoring, MIS, and reporting framework to track training coverage, effectiveness, 
and outcomes. 
d. Provide end-to-end support to ensure quality delivery, operational readiness, and field -level 
adoption.
